# Opportunities (https://thz-el.org/opportunities)

## Higher degree by research students (MPhil and PhD)
We are looking for self-motivated higher degree by research students to join our team.

For domestic applicants, as well as international applicants who have completed a relevant Australian or New Zealand qualification, see [Graduate Research Talent Scheme](https://adelaide.edu.au/research/research-degrees/research-scholarships/#:~:text=to%20apply.-,Graduate%20Research%20Talent%20Scheme,-The%20Graduate%20Research).

For international applicants with an oversea qualification, we might have a suitable scholarship available.

For either category above, if you are interested, send your CV and transcripts to withawat@adelaide.edu.au. Do not put in a file-sharing facility, as it will not be opened.

You can expect a reply within 24 hrs if your academic record is competitive for scholarship. Due to a volume of enquiries, unfortunately we cannot respond to every email. Thus, please do not resend email if you do not receive any response.

## Projects for coursework students
Coursework students who are interested in honours or master's projects are encouraged to reach out for a discussion prior to selecting their project. Owing to our vast activities around terahertz technology, from device design, sensing, to communications, we can tailor a project to match your interest and background.

Send your relevant transcript to withawat@adelaide.edu.au.

## Research experience for coursework students
Highly-motivated Adelaide students with strong passion in electromagnetics, sensing, or communications are welcome to contact us for discussion. Send your relevant transcript to withawat@adelaide.edu.au.
