## Terahertz Components

### Metasurfaces for full control of free-space waves
- Image: metasurfaces.webp
- Alt: Metasurface sample under test
- Text: A metasurface is a planar array of sub-wavelength metallic or dielectric resonators. Strong interaction between incident terahertz waves and these resonators gives us fine control over amplitude, phase, and polarisation, which is especially valuable where natural materials with the right properties are scarce. We apply this to beamforming, polarisation control, sensing, and dynamic wavefront manipulation.
- Tags: metasurfaces; metamaterials; frequency-selective surfaces; wavefront engineering; waveplates; flat optics; transmitarrays; reflectarrays

### High-efficiency broadband terahertz antennas & lenses
- Image: antennas.webp
- Alt: Broadband terahertz antenna and lens hardware
- Text: We design beamforming devices to meet demanding requirements in bandwidth, efficiency, directivity, compactness, and fabrication complexity. A key consideration is broadband operation, capitalising on the vast bandwidth available at terahertz frequencies, while mitigating the increasing ohmic losses that limit radiation efficiency. Our research focuses on unconventional antenna and lens designs that enable practical terahertz wireless and imaging applications.
- Tags: all-dielectric antennas; leaky-wave antennas; broadband; high gain; effective medium; GRIN optics; 3D printing; beam steering; free-form optics

### Substrateless integrated platform for terahertz frontends
- Image: substrateless.webp
- Alt: Substrateless silicon terahertz integrated platform
- Text: Terahertz technology is shifting from free-space optics towards integration, but high losses in metallic and dielectric materials rule out a direct adoption of conventional integrated-circuit technologies. We originated a substrateless platform of high-resistivity silicon based on effective medium theory, reaching arbitrary permittivity values with structural simplicity, near-zero dissipation, and fractional bandwidth exceeding 40%.
- Tags: terahertz integration; dielectric waveguides; photonic crystals; effective medium; multiplexers; filters; modulators

## Terahertz Systems

### Quadrature homodyne detection
- Image: homodyne.webp
- Alt: Quadrature homodyne detection system
- Text: Our terahertz sensing architectures provide robustness, high-sensitivity, and real-time response. Quadrature homodyne detection is central to this approach, suppressing phase noise while recovering both amplitude and phase responses of the target. This capability enables sensitive terahertz measurements ranging from nanoscale vibration to non-contact probing of active semiconductor devices.

## Terahertz Applications

### 6G communications and beyond
- Image: 6g.webp
- Alt: 6G communications research
- Text: Terahertz spectrum eases congestion at lower microwave and millimetre-wave bands, in theory supporting Tbit/s links over several kilometres. Line-of-sight terahertz propagation aligns well with the fixed, high-capacity links needed for dense urban base stations, last-mile connections, and data centres. We work on both the communications DSP and the hardware to push these links further.

### Non-destructive evaluation
- Image: nde.webp
- Alt: Non-destructive evaluation imaging setup
- Text: Terahertz waves penetrate dry, non-metallic materials, such as plastics, paper, cloth, and building materials, with millimetre spatial and depth resolution. We apply terahertz imaging techniques for quality control, security screening, gesture recognition, and medical diagnosis, across sectors including agriculture, wine, healthcare, and defence.

### Spectroscopy
- Image: spectroscopy.webp
- Alt: Terahertz spectroscopy setup
- Text: The terahertz band hosts rotational, vibrational, lattice, and plasma resonances distinctive of different materials. We combine spectroscopic techniques with precision metrology for high-fidelity material characterisation, sensing, and diagnostics.

### Astronomy and atmospheric sensing
- Image: astronomy.webp
- Alt: Illustrative map of the Antarctic ozone hole: low total ozone over the South Pole
- Text: Water vapour, ozone, carbon monoxide, and complex organics show their strongest rotational transitions in the terahertz band, tracing chemical abundance, temperature, and dynamics in planetary atmospheres and star-forming regions alike. We develop the low-loss beam-control structures and high-responsivity detectors that make this progress possible.
