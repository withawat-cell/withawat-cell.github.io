## End‑to‑End Wireless Communication Chain Simulator
Developed by [Marlon Kha](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marlon-kha-208141293/) (LinkedIn link)

This MATLAB application provides a flexible environment for modelling, testing, and demonstrating complete wireless communication chains. Developed to align with the Terahertz Engineering Laboratory's current transmission hardware capabilities, the tool enables researchers to explore system behaviour from baseband generation through to signal recovery.

The app supports both sinusoidal and complex waveform transmission, allowing users to prototype a wide range of modulation and signalling strategies. Built‑in digital compensation modules—including IQ‑imbalance correction and coarse time‑synchronisation—help users evaluate system robustness and isolate hardware‑induced impairments.

Designed as a standalone, user‑friendly interface, the simulator streamlines rapid experimentation, algorithm development, and hardware‑in‑the‑loop testing for high‑frequency wireless links. It serves as a practical platform for training, demonstration, and early‑stage research into advanced communication architectures.

Repository: https://github.com/withawat-cell/comm_chain.git

## CST Simulation of Substrateless Terahertz Waveguide
Python automation script for CST Studio Suite, compatible with Python 3.12.10 and Visual Studio Code, and CST Microwave Studio 2025. The code builds, configures, and runs full-wave transient simulations for substrateless (or effective-medium-clad) dielectric terahertz waveguides. The implementation and underlying structural parameters are based on the design principles demonstrated in the works in:
- W. Gao, X. Yu, M. Fujita, T. Nagatsuma, C. Fumeaux, and W. Withayachumnankul, "Effective-medium-cladded dielectric waveguides for terahertz waves," *Optics Express*, vol. 27, no. 26, pp. 38721–38734, 2019. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.382181
- W. Gao, W. S.-L. Lee, X. Yu, M. Fujita, T. Nagatsuma, C. Fumeaux, and W. Withayachumnankul, "Characteristics of effective-medium-clad dielectric waveguides," *IEEE Transactions on Terahertz Science and Technology*, vol. 11, no. 1, pp. 28–41, 2021. DOI (as linked on this page): https://doi.org/10.1109/TTHZ.2020.3023917 — NOTE: this differs from the DOI captured for the same citation on the Journal Articles page (https://doi.org/10.1109/TTHZ.2020.2964812 was used there for a different 2020 paper, and no DOI link at all was present for this exact citation on the Journal Articles page — only a plain https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9195766 link).
