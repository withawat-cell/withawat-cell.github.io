Opportunities

We are always keen to hear from motivated students interested in terahertz engineering.

Higher degree by research (MPhil / PhD)

We are looking for self-motivated higher degree by research students to join our team.

For domestic applicants, as well as international applicants who have completed a relevant Australian or New Zealand qualification, see the Graduate Research Talent Scheme.

For international applicants with an overseas qualification, we might have a suitable scholarship available.

If you are interested, send your CV and transcripts to withawat@adelaide.edu.au. Do not put it in a file-sharing facility, as it will not be opened.

You can expect a reply within 24 hours if your academic record is competitive for scholarship. Due to a volume of enquiries, unfortunately we cannot respond to every email, please do not resend if you do not receive a response.

Email your CV and transcripts

Projects & research experience for Adelaide University students

Coursework students interested in honours or master's projects are encouraged to reach out for a discussion prior to selecting their project. Owing to our vast activities around terahertz technology, from device design and sensing to communications, we can tailor a project to match your interest and background.

Highly-motivated Adelaide students with a strong passion for electromagnetics, sensing, or communications are also welcome to contact us about research experience opportunities.

Send your relevant transcript to withawat@adelaide.edu.au.
